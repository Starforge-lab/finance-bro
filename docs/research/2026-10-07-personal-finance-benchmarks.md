# Personal-finance benchmarks and development lessons

Researched: 7 October 2026. Scope: evidence to guide Finance Bro's existing manual-first, India/INR, local-only roadmap. This is a development reference, not a new feature specification or approval to change architecture.

Release scope was narrowed on 10 October 2026: [2.0 is Android SMS automatic imports only](https://github.com/Starforge-lab/finance-bro/issues/58). Broader product lessons remain reference or later backlog, not additional 2.0 gates. Documentation-drift observations below describe the research date, rather than the current repository state.

## Conclusion

Prioritize fast manual entry, understandable spending totals, easy corrections and user-controlled portability. Add automation only with visible outcomes and recovery paths. Competitors demonstrate useful mechanisms, but their popularity does not prove which mechanisms caused success. Finance Bro's low-effort experience still needs testing with its own users.

## Evidence and selection

The commercial sample covers established budgeting, manual tracking and Indian SMS tracking. The open-source sample covers a substantial budgeting project, mobile budgeting, manual local tracking and our existing Sushi reference. This is a purposive benchmark sample, not a market-share ranking or an exhaustive survey.

- Official help pages establish documented behavior and limitations; marketing establishes vendor claims, not independently verified outcomes.
- Google Play download bands and GitHub stars are adoption/interest proxies. Downloads are cumulative Android installs, not active users; stars are not installs. Counts below are snapshots and are not comparable across those measures.
- Public issues and reviews identify possible failure modes. They do not establish prevalence, current reproducibility or whether a later release fixed them.
- No competitor was installed or exercised. No comparable retention, task-time, error-rate or causal savings data was established. Avoid claims such as “this feature improves retention” until we measure them.
- Prices, platform support, maintenance status and store metrics must be refreshed before decisions that depend on them.

## Current gaps and proposals requiring a decision

Observed in the research checkout (`feat/paste-import`, commit `1253a71`): storage and paste-save APIs exist; custom ledger/review screens are not connected. These implementations are pending integration in [PR #30](https://github.com/Starforge-lab/finance-bro/pull/30) and [PR #31](https://github.com/Starforge-lab/finance-bro/pull/31), not implemented capabilities on `dev` at the time of this report. The schema has no opening balance or reconciliation fields. Paste dedupe is conservative and uses a non-cryptographic fallback; identical no-reference messages cannot distinguish a replay from a second genuine purchase. See the [ledger contract at the inspected revision](https://github.com/Starforge-lab/finance-bro/blob/1253a71d62c1004e387037c5a3d7dd526cf67da8/docs/ledger.md). Do not claim these gaps are solved by competitor research.

1. **Recovery before wider use with real financial data:** discuss bringing encrypted full backup/restore forward. Decide its format, included entities, key-loss behavior, tombstone/fingerprint handling and platform destination flows first. Restore into a fresh install must prove correctness. CSV alone does not cover this. This remains a proposal, not a reprioritized milestone.
2. **Balances and forecasts:** agree opening-balance/account-coverage requirements, credit-card liability treatment, eligible income/bills and estimation rules before implementation. Reconciliation can follow if users need bank-balance accuracy; it is not a reason to add a full accounting subsystem now.
3. **Repeated identical imports:** explain the present limitation and manual recovery. Determine whether real anonymized examples justify a user-controlled disambiguation path before changing identity logic.

Documentation drift observed: [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md) still says the license is undecided; CLAUDE/README/AGENTS and pending implementation branches describe different implementation or distribution states. Live [roadmap #7](https://github.com/Starforge-lab/finance-bro/issues/7) records the approved manual-first sequence and F-Droid/Apache-2.0/no-persisted-raw-message decisions pending documentation adoption. Resolve that drift in the relevant documentation work; this report does not silently replace it.

## Small validation loop

For each feature, identify the relevant lesson above, adapt it to agreed scope, and record observed results alongside the PR's validation. Evidence narrows the hypothesis; our tests determine whether it works for our users.

When the affected screens are available, run the same fictional tasks with Suvo, Rohan and willing additional testers: add an expense, fix its category, identify monthly overspend, find an overdue bill, review a duplicate paste and export records. Include Android/iOS and responsive web as applicable. No analytics SDK or remote collection is needed: use a local manual observation sheet.

Record task completion without help, elapsed seconds/taps, wrong interpretations, save/reopen failures, and reported friction. Record device/build and denominator (for example, 3 of 5 testers completed a task); a tiny convenience sample is usability feedback, not statistical proof. No benchmark results have been collected yet.

Release checks for the exercised scenarios: exact paise/month totals; no silent data loss or protected-record overwrite; no permission/account/network prerequisite for core manual flows; clear separation of recorded facts and estimates. Pick task-speed targets after a measured baseline rather than inheriting an unsupported “3 seconds” claim.

Refresh only the relevant competitor sources when a feature decision depends on them or a material market/platform change is reported. No scheduled competitor monitor or recurring automation was created.
